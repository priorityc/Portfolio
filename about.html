My Story

Running a business taught me one of the most important lessons: individuality is everything. I tried templates, quick fixes, and DIY solutions, but nothing felt authentic. That’s when I realised something powerful — a brand is like creating a person. It needs its own qualities, its own character, and its own voice. Without individuality, a business becomes invisible.

As a former small business owner, I understand how challenging it is to work with a limited budget while still wanting a professional digital home for your brand. That desire — to have a space that feels truly yours — was the spark that led me into the world of technology. I took my first technical step by enrolling in a bootcamp. It was small, but it changed everything. It strengthened my dedication and resilience and opened the door to a much longer journey. From there, I committed myself to learning the vast landscape of digital disciplines: problem‑solving, management, programming, and continuously improving my skills to reach a professional level.

Petya Digital Care

Petya Digital Care was born from the belief that every business deserves a brand that feels human, intentional, and unique. I support small businesses with a complete digital experience — from visual identity and logo design to modern websites and user‑focused digital solutions.

Brand Philosophy

I believe a brand is more than colours and shapes — it’s a living identity. A brand is like a person: it has a character, values, emotions, and a story. When a brand feels human, people connect with it. When it feels generic, people forget it.

Studio Values

  • Individuality first — every brand deserves its own personality.
  • Clarity over complexity — design should feel simple, not overwhelming.
  • Consistency builds trust — strong brands stay visually aligned.
  • Empathy in design — I design for real people, not trends.
  • Care in every detail — small businesses deserve thoughtful, high‑quality work.

Founder’s Message

I know how hard it is to build a business. I know how important it is to feel seen. And I know how powerful a strong identity can be. Petya Digital Care exists to help small businesses grow with confidence — through branding, design, and digital experiences that feel personal, modern, and meaningful.
