Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to the most common questions before starting a project.

What is included in a brand identity package?

Logo, colour palette, typography, brand elements, and brand guidelines. Logo design is also included.

How long does the brand identity process take?

Typically 7–12 days, depending on the complexity of your project.

How many logo concepts will I receive?

You’ll receive 2–3 initial logo concepts to choose from.

Can I request revisions on the designs?

Yes, of course. I provide revision rounds to ensure you love the final result.

What digital assets do you design?

Social media templates, flyers, posters, menus, banners, and more.

Do you provide editable templates?

Yes — Canva or Figma templates.

What is included in website design?

Homepage, inner pages, responsive layouts, UX/UI structure, and visuals.

Do you design mobile versions?

Yes — mobile‑first design is included and adapted to any screen size.

What platforms do you use?

WordPress, Wix, and custom front‑end builds.

Do you offer maintenance?

Yes — monthly maintenance plans are available for web design and development packages.

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes — every project starts with a free consultation and website audit on existing sites.

How do I start a project?

Send me a message and I’ll guide you through onboarding, or fill in my short initial form to receive a detailed proposal.
