Service Quote Calculator

Interactive mobile application walkthrough demonstrating automated pricing, dynamic input validation, and instant quote generation for construction services.

Application Walkthrough

1

Service Category Selection

Users select from predefined construction and home improvement services, dynamically loading the relevant pricing parameters.

2

Dynamic Measurement & Input

Real-time form validation ensures accurate area measurements, material tiers, and labour rates before running calculations.

3

Automated Cost Breakdown

Custom TypeScript pricing logic instantly computes material costs, labour hours, and optional add-ons without manual spreadsheets.

4

Instant Quote Summary

Generates a clean, itemized estimate ready to be reviewed on-site with clients, reducing quoting time and human error.
