I’m Petya - a Computing & IT graduate who spent the last four years building real projects across web, app, and cloud development. Along the way, I discovered how much I love blending technical problem‑solving with creative design. After choosing to change career a few years ago, I finally found the work that excites me: creating digital experiences that look great, work flawlessly, and genuinely help businesses to be recognisable and grow. Today, I combine IT expertise with graphic design to deliver complete, thoughtful solutions.

What is Petya Digital Care?

Petya Digital Care comes from my own journey as a small business owner. I’ve been through the struggle of trying to build a brand with limited time, limited budget, and endless uncertainty. I know how frustrating it is when your business has potential, but your identity doesn’t show it. That’s why individuality is at the heart of my studio. I design visual identities and digital tools that help small businesses express who they truly are with clearly, confidently, and creatively.
