I’m Petya — a Computing & IT graduate who spent the last four years building real projects across web, app, and cloud development. Along the way, I discovered how much I love blending technical problem‑solving with creative design.

What is Petya Digital Care?

Petya Digital Care comes from my own journey as a small business owner. I’ve been through the struggle of trying to build a brand with limited time, limited budget, and endless uncertainty.
