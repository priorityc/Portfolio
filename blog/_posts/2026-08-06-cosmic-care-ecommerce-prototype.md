## What is CosmicCare?

CosmicCare is a fully‑functional e‑commerce prototype I designed and developed from scratch.  
It started as a simple idea: **build a real product, not just a demo** — something that feels like a complete online store with a brand, identity, and user experience.

I created everything:

- the brand philosophy
- the logo
- the color system
- the UI/UX structure
- the product flow
- the checkout experience
- the backend simulation
- the real‑mode + demo‑mode architecture

CosmicCare became a high‑fidelity prototype that behaves like a real store while remaining safe for portfolio visitors.

---

## Dual‑Mode Architecture (Real + Demo)

One of the most unique features of CosmicCare is its **dual‑mode system**:

### **Real Mode**

- Live product data from Supabase
- Stripe checkout validation
- Real cart logic
- Real product syncing

### **Demo Mode**

- Safe mock data
- No real payments
- Perfect for recruiters browsing the store
- Identical UI/UX without risk

This dual‑mode setup makes CosmicCare both **functional** and **portfolio‑friendly**.

---

## Backend Simulation with Express.js

To make the store feel real, I built a lightweight backend simulation using Express.js.

It handles:

- checkout validation
- product syncing
- environment‑based configuration
- demo vs real mode switching

This allowed me to create a realistic shopping flow without exposing sensitive keys or requiring a full backend deployment.

## Mobile‑First UI/UX

CosmicCare is designed mobile‑first because most users shop on their phones.

I focused on:

- clean product cards
- intuitive cart interactions

- simple navigation

The UI is intentionally minimal, modern, and easy to use.
